Data & Definitions

What the dashboard publishes, how each comparison is calculated, and what to keep in mind.

Historical membership and units

my.Scouting National Membership and Total Units exports acquired November 6, 2026 cover December 2020, December 2021, and every month January 2022–November 2026. November 2026 is partial through November 6; December 2026 is unavailable. Youth measures count registrations, not distinct people.

Units are sums of exported detail, not verified distinct-unit counts. In 34 of 61 reports, detail sums differ from dashboard totals. No corrections were invented. Source numeric blanks were summed as zero within existing aggregates; missing council/program rows remain unavailable. Ships/Sea Scouts and Crews/Venturing are separated by explicit unit labels, with unspecified types separate.

Historical CST summaries use November 6, 2026 council assignments. They are not historical CST boundaries. Merger notes retain source links and qualified dates; predecessor and successor counts are never added together. Same-month growth uses paired current and prior counts with positive aggregate baselines. Missing observations are not interpolated. Stable-cohort program trends require each included council to have a youth value in every displayed period.

Privacy

Aggregate data only

The published JSON contains council-level totals and rates. Unit-level worksheet rows and local filesystem paths are deliberately excluded.

Growth

Consistent denominator

Year-over-year growth is calculated as (current youth − prior youth) ÷ prior youth. Year-end change uses 2025 year-end youth as its baseline.

Ranking

Higher growth ranks first

Rank 1 is the largest positive percentage change. Ties receive the same competition rank; the next rank advances by the number of tied councils.

CSTs

Source-assigned territories

Each council is grouped under its CST from the membership source. Territory totals sum the councils in view; growth is recalculated from summed current and baseline youth. TAY penetration includes only councils with a positive TAY denominator, using those same councils for youth membership. Search narrows summaries, charts, and council details together. Ranks in council details remain national.

Peers

Comparable scale

Peers are other councils whose unit count, current youth membership, and TAY population are each between 80% and 120% of the focus council.

Geography

Area and urban population

Square miles is matched ZIP land area; Urban % is the 2020 Census urban population share. Both are imported unchanged from the Councils worksheet, backed by Data_Census_Gov. They describe geographic context and do not affect growth ranks or peer selection. Missing source values display as unavailable.

TAY

Market context

TAY is published directly from the Councils worksheet. TAY penetration is calculated as current youth divided by TAY; rank 1 is the highest penetration.

Unit metrics

Workbook-defined signals

Unit health distributions and driver rates come from the workbook’s All, Pack, Troop, Crew, Post, and Ship metric tabs. National benchmarks are unit-weighted.

Data grain

Two populations

The ranking tabs and unit-metric tabs do not share identical aggregate youth and unit totals. Overview and Rankings use the council membership grain; Unit Health remains within the metric-tab population.

Imported continuation metric

Window unverified

The imported Unit Metrics field named “retention” follows the workbook model. Its cohort and measurement window are unverified, so it is not a verified annual cohort-retention measure. Source values above 100% remain unchanged.

Interpretation

Use rankings as a starting point

Rankings describe relative movement in this workbook snapshot; they do not by themselves explain causality, seasonal timing, local market conditions, or data-quality differences. Pair them with local operating context before making decisions.

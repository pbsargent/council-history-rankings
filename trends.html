Source, definitions and limitations

Historical source: my.Scouting National Membership and Total Units dashboard exports acquired November 6, 2026. Council/program aggregates only. December 2020 and December 2021 are annual points; monthly coverage begins January 2022. November 2026 is month-to-date. Missing council/program rows remain unavailable. Ships/Sea Scouts and Crews/Venturing are separated using explicit Ship/Crew unit labels within the source’s combined Crews category; other categories retain source labels. Unspecified unit types remain a separate bucket. Source summaries sum blank numeric detail cells as zero; those sums do not establish independent zero-member or zero-unit records.

*Unit measures are sums of exported detail, not verified distinct-unit counts. In 34 of 61 unit reports these sums differ from dashboard totals; no corrections were invented. Unit growth, unit-based scatter positions and youth-per-unit ratios are provisional.

Peer sets use the original November 6 ranking snapshot’s ±20% units/youth/TAY rule and stay fixed as the historical month changes. Snapshot CST/TAY are context only. There is no historical TAY penetration or cohort retention calculation. Benchmarks recompute growth from summed counts; indexed series use January 2022 = 100. Group series require the same councils to have values for every month from January 2022 through the selected month, so coverage is stable within each view.

Names are current archive aliases matched by council number. Historical geography may be remapped. Merger notes are research with qualified dates; no predecessor/successor counts are added, and partial transfers are not whole-council rollups. Ranks are descriptive, not merger-adjusted performance assessments.

Original ranking and Unit Health pages remain a separately sourced November 6, 2026 snapshot. Their source populations and definitions differ from this historical archive.
